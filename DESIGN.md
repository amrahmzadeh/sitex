## Brand & Style

The brand personality embodies poise, vocal authority, resonant warmth, and cultural distinction. Designed for an esteemed Azerbaijani speech culture expert, master of ceremonies, and public voice trainer, the interface conveys an atmosphere of high-end editorial curation reminiscent of luxury print journals and bespoke academy ateliers. 

Targeted toward corporate executives, media personalities, high-profile event organizers, and aspiring public speakers, the experience evokes immediate trust, intellectual prestige, and stage-lit confidence. The design combines an editorial layout with tailored minimalism: expansive canvas margins, strict asymmetric balance, deliberate typographic drama, and tactile champagne gold accents.

## Colors

The palette establishes an executive, warm, and cinematic tone through balanced light-mode contrast:

- **Canvas Background (`#FAF7F2`)**: Warm ivory foundation serving as the primary backdrop, avoiding clinical starkness while evoking fine-art paper.
- **Primary Navy (`#0F1B2D`)**: Deep midnight navy utilized for authoritative headers, high-contrast visual anchors, formal evening sections, and dominant iconography.
- **Secondary Champagne Gold (`#C9A66B`)**: Radiant metallic accent applied to calls-to-action, active indicators, selective ornamental borders, and focus rings.
- **Tertiary Muted Gold (`#E8D7B8`)**: Understated parchment gold dedicated to secondary borders, tag containers, badges, and card divider lines.
- **Neutral Charcoal (`#1E1E1E`)**: Refined charcoal chosen over pitch black for editorial body reading clarity and reduced eye strain.
- **Surface Layer (`#FFFFFF`)**: Pure crisp white reserved for lifted content cards, masterclass dossiers, and layered testimonial modules.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the literary high-contrast grace of Playfair Display with the clean geometric functionalism of Manrope. 

- **Display & Headlines (Playfair Display)**: Brings vocal cadence and prestige to page titles, personal statements, and section milestones. Headlines leverage open leading to maintain an editorial magazine posture. Italicized styling is used selectively for vocal emphasis and Azeri poetic rhythm.
- **Body & Captions (Manrope)**: Ensures optimal legibility on both mobile touch devices and high-density desktop monitors. Its neutral geometric skeleton counters the ornate serif headings, keeping long-form session syllabi and booking descriptions crisp.
- **Labels & Overlines (Manrope)**: Set in all-caps with generous letter spacing (`0.08em` to `0.1em`) to frame sections, categorize service pillars, and guide user navigation with architectural discipline.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a 12-column desktop grid (max-width: 1320px) transitioning to an 8-column layout on tablet and a 4-column layout on mobile devices.

- **Generous Editorial Margins**: Desktop viewports feature large margins (`space-2xl` to `space-3xl`) around major milestones to create an expansive, serene viewing cadence with visual breathing room.
- **Asymmetric Offsets**: Text blocks sit beside portraiture and video showreels with intentional structural offsets, reinforcing an editorial magazine feel over a rigid SaaS dashboard structure.
- **Mobile Reflow**: On mobile viewports (`< 768px`), gutters reduce to `1rem`, outer canvas margins collapse to `1.25rem`, and multi-column service grids stack cleanly into cohesive vertical stories.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses ambient diffusion rather than heavy physical elevation, treating the interface like layered sheets of warm luxury paper and matte gallery surfaces.

- **Surface Levels**:
  - *Base Layer*: Canvas background (`#FAF7F2`).
  - *Layer 1 (Cards & Modules)*: Crisp pure white surfaces (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px border rendered in muted gold (`#E8D7B8` at 60% opacity).
  - *Layer 2 (Elevated Flyouts & Dialogs)*: Floating surfaces elevated with subtle ambient shadows.
- **Shadow Profiles**:
  - *Subtle Card Rest*: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(15, 27, 45, 0.04)`
  - *Card Hover & Lift*: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(15, 27, 45, 0.08)`
  - *Signature Floating Nav / Modal*: `0 20px 48px -8px rgba(15, 27, 45, 0.12)`
- **Midnight Accents**: Dark thematic blocks (e.g., keynote showreel sections and voiceover studios) invert into solid midnight navy (`#0F1B2D`) with subtle inner gold borders, creating intentional dramatic contrast against the ivory background.

## Shapes

The interface balances sharp editorial discipline with accessible elegance by standardizing on a 12px (`0.75rem`) border radius across all primary cards, modal modules, and media wrappers.

- **Cards & Media Frames**: Standardized at 12px to soften large media previews, headshots, and event portfolios without rounding them into casual or child-like forms.
- **Interactive Badges & Buttons**: Interactive pills or softly curved elements use 8px (`0.5rem`) on buttons, while status indicators and accent tags retain fully rounded pills for distinct visual contrast against structured structural cards.
- **Borders**: All card frames feature a delicate 1px border line using champagne gold tints (`#E8D7B8`) to provide crisp definition against warm ivory backgrounds.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Qeydiyyatdan Keç / Müraciət Et)**: Background in rich champagne gold (`#C9A66B`), typography in midnight navy (`#0F1B2D`) using Manrope SemiBold with uppercase tracking. Hover state shifts to `#B8955A` with subtle lift.
- **Secondary Action (Haqqında / Ətraflı Oxu)**: Ghost border of 1.5px in midnight navy (`#0F1B2D`) or champagne gold (`#C9A66B`), transparent interior, text color matching the border. Hover triggers a subtle tinted fill.
- **Dark-Section Buttons**: Solid ivory surface with midnight navy text, accented by a gold halo on hover.

### Cards
- **Service & Training Modules (Nitq Mədəniyyəti, Diksiya, Aparıcılıq)**: Pure white canvas (`#FFFFFF`), 12px border radius, 1px perimeter border in `#E8D7B8`, internal padding set to `space-xl`. Top-accented with an editorial category label in champagne gold.
- **Testimonial Dossiers**: Deep ivory or pure white card with an oversized quotation mark in champagne gold, containing participant credentials and corporate seals.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Input Fields (Ad, Soyad, Əlaqə Nömrəsi, Təlim Növü)**: Background `#FAF7F2`, thin border in `#E8D7B8`, text in `#1E1E1E`. On focus: transitions to 1.5px border in `#C9A66B` with a soft gold focus glow.
- **Dropdowns & Pickers**: Clean, minimalist white menu sheets with 12px roundedness, 1px gold outline, and subtle navy checkmarks.

### Chips & Filter Tags
- **Category Chips (Masterklass, Fərdi Dərslər, Korporativ)**: Border in `#E8D7B8`, background in transparent or `#FFFFFF`. Active state uses solid `#0F1B2D` with `#FAF7F2` text and gold border tint.

### Audio & Voice-Over Player Component
- Custom player for vocal reels and voiceover clips: Horizontal card with midnight navy surface (`#0F1B2D`), gold waveform visualizer, pure white playback timecode, and a circular champagne gold play/pause button.

### Event & Experience Timeline
- Minimalist vertical timeline tracking notable emcee appearances and public lectures: Central hairline in `#E8D7B8`, milestones marked with 8px gold nodes, accompanied by date callouts in Playfair Display.
